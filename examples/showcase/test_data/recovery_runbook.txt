RECOVERY RUNBOOK (illustrative)

1. Retrieve the sealed archive from the vault. The envelope is labelled with
   the check value of the key that opens it. Confirm it matches the value in
   the ceremony record before going further.

2. Convene two custodians. Neither may hold the archive and the key at the
   same time between the vault and the room.

3. Open the archive on the air-gapped machine. Follow the steps inside from
   the top; do not skip to the signing section.

4. Record the time the archive was opened and by whom, and re-seal what is
   left at the end of the session.
